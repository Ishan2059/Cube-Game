CubeCrush cosmetic skin concepts

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. The exact prompt set is in prompts.json.
Ten original 1254 x 1254 PNGs with alpha transparency. These are presentation
renders for the Figma shop and skin previews, not finished 3D meshes or animated
game materials. Cosmetic concepts preserve the cube silhouette and do not imply
changes to player speed, damage, hitboxes, or leaderboard scoring.

Magma: obsidian and molten seams
Boba Buddy: caramel milk tea and tapioca pearls
Gummy Pop: translucent sugar-coated candy
Glacier: frosted blue ice
Mossling: bark, moss, and tiny mushrooms
Arcade: retro robot with pixel eyes
Nebula: cosmic glass with stars and purple clouds
Honey Drop: amber honeycomb
Tidepool: aquarium cube with a tiny fish
Prism: iridescent chrome
