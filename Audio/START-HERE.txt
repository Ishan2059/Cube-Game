CUBECRUSH — TACTILE ARCADE SOUND PACK

Open preview.html to audition all cues and their variations.
The two preview mixes demonstrate gameplay layering and all 14 creature kills.
Use headphones or speakers; the preview begins at 65% volume.

wav/       48 kHz, 24-bit PCM WAV masters, mono for positional/repeated effects.
ogg/       Compact Ogg Vorbis alternatives. WAV is the compatibility fallback.
previews/  Mixed examples, not individual game assets.
manifest.json       Every cue, variant, intended trigger, priority and cooldown.
integration-map.json Existing sound functions mapped to the replacement cues.
mixing-guide.json    Voice limits, suppression rules and implementation notes.
validation.json     Signal checks and decoding results for every exported asset.
tools/              Deterministic synthesis source and pinned Python dependencies.

Sound direction: tactile stone movement, playful liquid splats, brittle shell
breaks, restrained UI feedback and soft mallet rewards. Creature kills have
three variations each. Score, coin and XP use different timbres.

These are original procedurally synthesized sounds, with no third-party samples
or recordings. They are not field recordings or voice performances.

The pack is ready for audition and integration. Creating it does not change
game/audio.ts or the live game's playback behavior. Most UI cues and XP cues
are new hooks for the redesign. Optional hover, ambient creature movement and
status ticks should start disabled to keep repeated play comfortable.

Rebuild:
  python -m venv .venv
  .venv/Scripts/python -m pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
  .venv/Scripts/python tools/build_audio.py

The build overwrites this pack's generated files with the same deterministic
recipes. Keep custom revisions outside the generated wav/ and ogg/ filenames.
